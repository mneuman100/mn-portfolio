Review Timesheets

Once time is reviewed, you can now review each Timesheet.

  1. Select Time & Expense > My approvals.

  2. From the Summary Tab, select: Time Sheets Pending Approval

  3. Click on the date range for each employee to drill into their Timesheet.

Review Lunch Ticket and Admin Wrap Entries

  1. In each Timesheet, verify that the Lunch Ticket entries = zero time (It will be in red if correct.)

  2. Then verify the length of each Admin Wrap entry. These are Identified on the Timesheet as:

    • Company= "Tech Corp."

    • Description= "Admin"

  3. Any Admin Wraps greater than an hour will be dealt with by the Manager, as they see fit.

Search for Tickets in "Enter Time/Fix Ticket" Status

Now, make sure no Tickets are in the "Enter Time/Fix Ticket" status.

  1. Navigate to Service Desk > Service Board > Service Ticket Search.

  2. In the Status field, select "Time/Fix Ticket".

  3. Then click Search.

  4. Reject any ticket entries that are displayed in the Search Results. (see the section "Reject Incorrect Timesheets" below)

Verify xxxxxxx xxxxxxxx Hours

Verify that the total hours in the xxxxxxx xxxxxxxx app are less than or equal to what is in the xxxxxxxxxxx app.

  1. Navigate to: [https:// xxxxx. xxxxxxxxxxxxxx.com/signin] (See the xx xxxx app for credentials.)

  2. Click "View Time" for employee.

  3. Select the Date Filter to match the pay period being reviewed.

  4. Note the total Billable Hours.

  5. Now, run the Time & Expense Report in the xxxxxxxxx app and note those total billable hours. (See "Review Time," above, for instructions on how to access this report.)

  6. After you have both counts, compare the two total hours. The xxxxxxx xxxxxxxx app hours should be less than or equal to what is in the xxxxxxxxxx app.

  7. If it is not, then reject their Timesheet and remind them to edit as needed to make the hours and timesheet match. (see the section "Reject Incorrect Timesheets" below)

Approve Timesheet

Once a Timesheet passes the above review process with no errors, you can approve the Timesheet.

  1. Select Time & Expense > My Approvals.

  2. From the Summary Tab, select: Time Sheets Pending Approval

  3. Click on the date range for each employee to drill into their Timesheet.

  4. Double-check for errors on the Timesheet:

    • If there ARE errors, skip to the next section, to reject the Timesheet.

    • If there are NO errors, select the "approve" button.
