Performing a document review (reviewer task)

You can send your external users the following instructions on how to perform a document review. We suggest also sending the corresponding "xxxxxx xxxx screen" topic.

To print this topic, select the print icon (image3.png) in the top-right corner.

>View the xxxxxx xxxx screen topic

You have been assigned to review a document. Once your review is complete, the document owner is notified.

To perform a document review

The following procedure is performed by the assigned reviewer.

  1. From your email system, open the reviewer assignment email and select the link.
    The xxxxxx xxxx screen displays.

  1. If you were sent a secure email, follow the Click here to retrieve your secure email link, then do one of the following.

    • If you need to register to access the secure email, on the Register for Secure Email screen, enter your information and then select Register.

    • If you already have a secure email password, log in, if needed.

      The Emails screen may display, which is your inbox and shows all of your secure email assignments. In the list, for the email you want to review, select the Details link.

      The Secure Email screen displays which shows the details of the email.
      To start the review, in Body select the link.

  1. Review the document and add comments or edits, as needed.

    If you get the User Assigned Action Error message, you may have already performed the review on this document or the link has expired.

    The xxxxxx xxxx screen displays.
    >Get detailed information about this screen

  1. To approve or reject the document, do one of the following:

    • If you agree with how the document is written and do not want to make any more changes, select Approve.

    • If you want to send your comments or document changes back for review, select Reject.
      Your review may be complete.

  1. If you were also asked to sign the review, after you Approve or Reject the document, the Sign Review screen displays. Do the following:

    1. Select Edit Signature and use your mouse to draw your signature.

    2. Select xxxxxxxx xxxxx.
      Your signature displays.

    3. Select Submit.
      Your review is complete.
