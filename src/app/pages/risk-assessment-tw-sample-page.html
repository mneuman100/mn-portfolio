Identify the Scope & Lens

First, identify what you are going to assess (the scope), being mindful of any regulatory or budgetary requirements. Usually, these are the critical processes from your BIA.

Then, determine the view by which you will analyze the threat events (the lens). Here are some lens examples:

  • by critical process

  • by location (offices, cloud based, divisions, etc.)

  • by department (can span multiple locations)

  • by system (infrastructure, servers, workstations, etc.)

  • by data type (customer, security, internal, etc.)

Note:The lens can change depending on what triggers the risk assessment. Triggers can include a new location, high staff turnover, a new critical process, etc.

Create your Risk Assessment Worksheet

The worksheet you create should (1) list all possible threat events for your chosen lens, (2) include a defined rating system for likelihood and impact, and (3) list the vulnerabilities and recommendations for each threat event.

Tip:The provided Risk Assessment Worksheet Template may be ready to use as is or can be customized as needed.

Identify Potential Threat Events

Brainstorm possible threat events using past experiences, team knowledge, and industry experts. Be forward-thinking, taking into account: changes in customer and industry trends, new technologies, future projects and processes, potential collateral damage due to your location, and potential changes to regulations/standards.

Create a Rating System

Create a rating system to evaluate each threat event by their likelihood and impact level. This should be a general rating scale, not exact likelihoods.

Rating system example:

Likelihood Scale Example:
1 = Improbable
2 = Unlikely
3 = Reasonably Possible
4 = Likely
5 = Very Likely/Expected		 Impact Scale Example:
1 = Insignificant
2 = Minor
3 = Moderate
4 = Major
5 = Catastrophic

Create a Risk Matrix and Determine your Risk Tolerance Threshold

Next, create a risk matrix that will be used to weigh your threat event based on its likelihood and impact levels. This matrix will also show your company's Risk Tolerance Threshold.
