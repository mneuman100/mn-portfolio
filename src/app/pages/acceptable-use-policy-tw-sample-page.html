Acceptable Use Policy

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish acceptable and unacceptable use of electronic devices and network resources at XYZ Company in conjunction with its established culture of ethical and lawful behavior, openness, trust, and integrity.

This policy is in place to protect the user and XYZ Company. Inappropriate use exposes XYZ Company to risks including virus attacks, compromise of network systems and services, and legal issues.

Effective security is a team effort involving the participation and support of every XYZ Company employee and affiliate who deals with information and/or information systems. It is the responsibility of every user to know these guidelines, and to conduct their activities accordingly.

Scope

This policy applies to Company Electronic Resources, which are owned or leased by XYZ Company, and include, but are not limited to: internet/intranet/extranet/cloud-based systems, computer equipment, software, operating systems, storage media, services, and network accounts providing electronic mail, web browsing and FTP.

This policy applies to the users of Company Electronic Resources, which include, but are not limited to XYZ Company employees, contractors, consultants, temporaries, and other workers at XYZ Company, including all personnel affiliated with third parties.

This policy applies to the use of information, electronic and computing devices, and network resources to conduct XYZ Company business or interact with internal networks and business systems, whether owned or leased by XYZ Company, the user, or a third party.

This policy lists activities that are prohibited. Certain XYZ Company employees may be exempted from these restrictions during their legitimate job responsibilities (e.g., systems administration staff may have a need to disable the network access of a host if that host is disrupting production services).

This policy is complementary to any previously implemented policies dealing specifically with data access, data storage, data movement, and connectivity of devices to Company Electronic Resources.
