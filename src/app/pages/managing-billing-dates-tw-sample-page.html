Managing billing dates

You can manage your billing dates on the billing profile and in the rating program.

For account level billing, you can manage the billing dates in billing settings.

> Learn how to manage the billing dates for account level billing

> Learn more about the Invoice management module

You can do the following:

  • > Manage billing dates on a billing profile

  • > Manage billing dates on a rating program

To manage billing dates on a billing profile

  1. At the client level, from the main menu, select Admin.

  1. In the xxxxxx xxxxxxx section, select billing profile.
    The Billing Profiles index screen displays.

  1. In the list, select the name of the billing profile you want to edit.
    The Billing Profile view screen displays.

  1. Select Edit Billing Profile.
    The billing profile screen displays.
    > Get detailed information about this screen

  1. Modify any of the following fields in the xxxxxx xxxxxxx section:

    • Days Notice: The number of days between the date on which the invoice is sent and the date on which the invoice needs to be paid.

    • First Invoice Due Today or On The Policy's Effective Date: Select this option to override Days Notice. When selected, the invoice is due as soon as the policy is effective.

    • Due Date: The number of days between the billing period start date or policy effective date, and the date on which the invoice needs to be paid.

    • Endorsement Invoice Due Date: The number of days between an endorsement generating an invoice and the date on which the invoice needs to be paid.

    • Down Payment Due: The number of days between the start of the billing period and the date on which the down payment needs to be paid.

    • Exclude Days: Indicates if the invoice date is adjusted to the previous day when the invoice date falls on an excluded day.

    • Holidays: Indicates if the invoice date is adjusted to the previous day when the invoice date falls on a holiday or non-work day.

    • End of Month: Indicates if the due date is adjusted to the last day of the month.

    • Due Date Adjustment: The maximum number of days by which the due date can be manually adjusted.

    • Adjustment Role: The roles who have permission to adjust the due date by the number of days specified in Due Date Adjustment.

  1. Modify any of the following fields in the xxxxxx xxxxxxx Options section:

    • Use effective date as due date: Determines if the Origami Risk system will uses the policy's effective day as the bill due day of the parent billing account.

    • Catch-up invoicing if first invoice is delayed: Determines if catch-up invoicing is used when the first invoice is delayed.

  1. Select Save Changes.

To manage billing dates on a rating program

  1. From the main menu, select Admin.

  1. In the Policies and Underwriting section, select Rating Programs.
    The Rating Programs index screen displays.

  1. In the list, select the rating program you want to edit.
    The rating program view screen displays.

  1. Select Edit Rating Program.

  1. Select More, and in the Rating Program section, select Billing and Commissions.

  1. Modify the following field:

    • Agency Bill Due: Indicates how many days after generating an invoice the payment can be collected without penalty.

  1. Select Save Changes.
