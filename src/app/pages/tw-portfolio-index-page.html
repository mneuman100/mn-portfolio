Hi, I'm Meghan!

I enjoy digging deep into the subject or software and building strong relationships with subject matter experts so I can understand more than just the stated procedure. I want to know the full user journey, including where people are likely to stumble.

I'm always thinking of my audience, and gently share usability issues and pain points that I've found to the product managers and engineering teams for their review.
